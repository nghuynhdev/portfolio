# Blog post covers

Current generation: **v2**, generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool after reading all five articles and their English translations in full. Final format: 1600 × 900 WebP. Both language versions explicitly select the same `-v2.webp` cover in frontmatter, avoiding cached versions of previously replaced files. The original unversioned cover files were removed after verifying the replacements, as requested.

Exact prompts, bilingual section coverage, article fingerprints, alternative concepts, technical caveats and review notes are in [BRIEFS-v2.json](./BRIEFS-v2.json). [PROMPTS.json](./PROMPTS.json) identifies the active assets.

Each article has its own visual treatment:

- 01: Warm paper and ultramarine search interface; `bao mat` finds the original accented `Bảo mật`.
- 02: Detailed browser game-editor scene with a selected platform and a matching playable preview.
- 03: Industrial process cutaway; the app is live while readiness fails at its required database connection.
- 04: Architectural ownership boundaries; forward proxy belongs with clients, reverse proxy with origins.
- 05: Editorial investigation collage distinguishing a traffic spike from suspicious backlink relationships.

These are conceptual illustrations, including the editor UI, not actual product screenshots or measurements. All five were inspected at full resolution and as 320 × 180 thumbnails before replacing the previous covers. Browser validation is recorded in the brief manifest.
